A The problem

Scientific knowledge is abundant. Turning it into working models without hallucination is hard.

Generic LLMs are dangerous in physical bioprocess engineering: they guess kinetic constants, invent yield parameters, and silently substitute incompatible equations, ruining expensive experimental runs.

THE GENERATIVE LLM TRAP

Silent Hallucinations in the Wet Lab

When prompted for bioprocess recipes, generic language models hallucinate plausible-looking numbers: they fabricate non-existent enzyme kinetics, invent conversion rates, and silently blend incompatible protocols across distinct bacterial strains. In physical scale-up, ungrounded synthetic parameters lead to failed batches and wasted capital.

FAILURE MODE: SILENT CONTAMINATION
THE SCALEUP AI PROTOCOL

Strict Fail-Closed Compilation

ScaleUp AI enforces an unbroken chain of custody. Every unit operation, boundary condition, and reaction constant is bound to DOI-verified literature with verbatim quotes. If an essential parameter is absent from the peer-reviewed evidence, the compiler raises an explicit InsufficientEvidenceError and halts execution.

PROTOCOL: DETERMINISTIC VERIFICATION

D Multi-domain digital twin explorer · domain two

From scientific literature to executable models: E. coli fed-batch replay

The demonstration below is captured from the ScaleUp AI Godot prototype: a desktop application that composes scientific process models through typed ports and runs them in dependency order, with a 3D evidence replay of the Zieliński et al. (2019) recombinant human insulin process.

TYPED PORTS

Compose models

Registered models (Zielinski insulin process, Ganjave, Morris material balance) are added from a palette and connected through typed, unit-aware ports. Required inputs have no defaults: a missing value blocks execution.

VALIDATION

Run and validate

A graph can be validated into an execution plan, or executed. Cycles are rejected, and each model runs in its own interpreter subprocess because their packages cannot coexist.

FAIL-CLOSED

Replay evidence

The 3D replay restates numbers read from a published paper and the accounting identities it supports. It reports no growth kinetics, no oxygen-transfer data, no AU calibration and no purification model - and declares those absences explicitly.

OPENALEX / CROSSREF

Investigate gaps

Under each missing quantity an Investigate button opens a panel: the model and run it was checked against, the gap in the model's own words, candidate findings from the literature with verbatim quoted passages, a dimension-by-dimension condition comparison, a verdict (incompatible / needs checking / compatible but not yet validated), and ten checkable validation criteria.

STRAIN BRIDGING

Check compatibility

A model registry records every model with a stable id, version, digests, ports and units. A compatibility engine assesses whether two models may be composed, with refused pairs reporting the specific difference and what would resolve it.

VERBATIM CITATIONS

Preserve provenance

Every displayed value carries a plain-language provenance label: stated in the source, read from a published figure, calculated from the available data, or not established by the available evidence.

E Scientific provenance deep-dive

Why cross-strain compatibility and mass transfer provenance matter

ScaleUp AI began with a concrete engineering goal: modelling recombinant human insulin production using E. coli. This proof of concept established our zero-hallucination guardrails and evidence tracking.

UNKNOWN QUANTITIES

What the model exposes

The paper reports agitation, aeration, dissolved-oxygen control, pressure, and exact feed profiles only partially - or not at all. The model records these as missing information rather than filling them in, so an engineer can see exactly what is unknown before attempting any prediction.

STRAIN BRIDGES

What a strain difference means

A finding reported for a different E. coli strain does not by itself prove evidence invalid. It introduces a compatibility question - same organism, different background - that must be evaluated before the evidence is reused or transferred.

SYSTEM INTEGRITY

Why this matters

Preserving evidence and surfacing unresolved questions is the value: the system reports what it does not know, so a human can decide whether a cross-strain result applies, instead of silently adopting it.
