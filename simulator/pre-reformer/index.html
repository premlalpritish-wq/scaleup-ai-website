Critical Audit Finding: WGS Enthalpy Discrepancy (Eq. 2.51 vs. NIST Standard)

Report Internal Discrepancy: In Samar's report, Equation 2.51 (p. 23) and Appendix B5-C write the heat of the Water-Gas Shift reaction as:
ΔHr₂ = -3.75 × 10⁵ kJ/kmol (-375 kJ/mol).
However, standard thermodynamic data (NIST / Perry's) for WGS (CO + H₂O ⇌ CO₂ + H₂) at 370°C is approximately:
ΔHr₂ = -3.75 × 10⁴ kJ/kmol (-37.5 kJ/mol) (an order-of-magnitude 10× difference).

• Appendix B5-3 Euler Basis: When executing with Samar's exact written value (-3.75e5 kJ/kmol), the reverse WGS reaction over-extracts heat, cooling the bed from its initial methanation peak (398.2°C at z=0.02m) back down to ~369.4–384.6°C at the exit.
• Corrected NIST / Stream 8 Basis: When using the true thermodynamic enthalpy (-3.75e4 kJ/kmol), the bed retains its exothermic heat release, reconciling the Euler calculation with Samar's Chapter 1/2 Stream 8 equilibrium outlet specification of 424.0°C.
