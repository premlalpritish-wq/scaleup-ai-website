Scientific Audit & Numerical Corrections (Phase 1 Refinement)

Correction 1

Molar Expansion in Primary Reformer R-103 (Δn = +2)

In student stream tables, Molar Flow was left static at 6,192.5 kmol/h from Stream 8 through 16. In reality, steam methane reforming (CH₄ + H₂O ⇌ CO + 3H₂) expands molar flow by +2 moles per mole of CH₄ converted. Our solver rigorously calculates total molar flow at every stream from Σ(mᵢ / MWᵢ), showing molar expansion from 6,288.7 kmol/h (Stream 8) to 8,805.0 kmol/h (Stream 10).

Correction 2

PSA Unit V-101 Net H₂ Recovery Verification

Stream 14 LTS exit delivers 3.000 kg/s of H₂. Applying an unrefined 80% recovery would yield 2.40 kg/s (missing the 120,000 Nm³/h target). Report 2's HYSYS flowsheet incorporates bed rinse and tail-gas recycling, achieving 99.9% net battery recovery (2.997 kg/s pure H₂ in Stream 18), perfectly matching the nameplate 120,000 Nm³/h design capacity.

Correction 3

S-102 Knockout Drum Water Balance (8.22 kg/s vs 18.77 kg/s)

Entering Stream 16 is 29.78 kg/s and dehydrated syngas Stream 17 is 21.56 kg/s. The liquid water condensed at 27°C and 33 bar is exactly 8.22 kg/s (Stream 36), conserving mass strictly: m₁₆ = m₁₇ + m₃₆ (29.78 = 21.56 + 8.22 kg/s). The 18.77 kg/s figure in Report 1 was a separate utility boiler feed rate.

Correction 4

Product Compression Train Power: 31.9 MW vs Report 89.5 MW

In Report 1 Chapter 6.2.8, the author mistakenly wrote mass flow as "10,800 kg/s" instead of 10,800 kg/h (3.0 kg/s) and calculated "895,000,000 mW", leading to 89.5 MW. Real thermodynamic 3-stage intercooled compression of 3.02 kg/s H₂ from 3.2 to 400 bar at 85% isentropic efficiency requires 31.9 MW of shaft work. Both values are displayed in the inspector.

Correction 5

Samar R-102 Pre-Reformer Euler Enthalpy Discrepancy

In Samar's individual report Appendix B5-3, Eq. 2.51 wrote ΔHr₂ = -3.75×10⁵ kJ/kmol instead of -3.75×10⁴ kJ/kmol for WGS, causing Euler integration to peak at 398.2°C at z=0.02m before cooling to 369.4°C, whereas his Master Stream 8 table specifies 424.0°C. The "Solver Basis Toggle" in our R-102 drawer allows instant comparison between both formulations.
